How I Work

No magic, just a habit of asking why before deciding how.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Who uses this, what are they trying to get done, and where do they get stuck today?

    “Why are we doing it this way?”
  2. 02

    Simplify

    Strip the problem down to what matters, then map the flow and the information before touching visuals.

    Less to think about, not less to do.
  3. 03

    Design

    Wireframes first, then high-fidelity screens and a consistent system so every page feels like the same product.

    Every decision has a reason.
  4. 04

    Build & refine

    Prototype or build it, put it in front of real people, and keep iterating on what they actually do.

    Shipped beats perfect.
